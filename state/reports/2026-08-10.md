## Tool errors / notes

- **Ad slot / creative-kind constraint (resolved).** The first `save_post` was
  rejected: "No 'primary' ad slot is available in this post." The publication's
  ad network permits only ONE "primary" (with-logo) ad per post. I kept the
  highest-value ad (Q3 Advisors) as primary and switched slots 2 and 3 to the
  "secondary" (no-logo) creative, which every offer supports. The re-save
  succeeded with all three ads accepted. **Lesson for future runs: at most one
  advertisementOpportunity per post may be `primary`; place additional ads as
  `secondary`.**
- Ad offer IDs rotate on every `list_ad_offers` call (as noted last run). I
  re-fetched immediately before each save attempt and used the fresh IDs.
- `save_image` requires HTTPS and does not accept spaces in the URL. Two of the
  three Wikimedia uploads failed on the first try because the Special:FilePath
  URLs contained literal spaces; percent-encoding the spaces (%20) fixed them.
  The Colombia image also hit a transient Wikimedia 429; retrying with a
  different Casa de Narino file succeeded.
- WebFetch was egress-blocked on themoscowtimes.com and maritime-executive.com;
  worked around using other credible sources (Foreign Policy, Washington Times,
  Reuters via MarineLink, and search summaries of the Reuters exclusive).
