  not accept any more opportunities." The ad network caps the number of
  concurrently accepted opportunities (13 already accepted on this
  publication). Passing fresh offer IDs from list_ad_offers made save_post try
  to accept new opportunities and hit the cap. list_ad_opportunities
  (status=accepted) showed Tello, Morning Brew, Superhuman AI, and Finance
  Advisors were **already accepted**. Fix: embed the existing accepted
  opportunity UUIDs directly (the advertisementOpportunity node accepts either
  an offer id or an accepted opportunity id). Second save succeeded with all 3
  ads placed. **Lesson for future runs: prefer list_ad_opportunities
  (status=accepted) UUIDs when offers are already accepted; only fall back to
  offer IDs for genuinely new, unaccepted offers.**
