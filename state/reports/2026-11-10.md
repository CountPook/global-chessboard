constraints (logged, non-fatal):

   ONE primary ad slot per post. Fix: one 'primary' (the top ad), the rest

   list_ad_offers tries to accept NEW opportunities, but the publication already
   held its full set of accepted opportunities. Fix: embed the already-accepted
   opportunity IDs from list_ad_opportunities(status=accepted) directly; those
   IDs are stable (unlike rotating offer IDs).
